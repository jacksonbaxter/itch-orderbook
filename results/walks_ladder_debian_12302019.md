# The walks of the backtester's simulated levels, timed on a ladder of schedules, on debian, 12302019

Generated by `scripts/walk_driver.py` from raw records that are not committed. Do not edit by hand.

Host: **debian**. Numbers from the two hosts are never mixed.

T-BT-3's engine and extract, timed by `bench_backtest --workload shadow` on schedules of shadow orders that differ only in each order's life, so that the longer lives hold more orders on more simulated levels and every walk from a side's best level has further to go. Each schedule's walks were counted once by `bench_walks` on this host, untimed, and every timed process gave its count's event hash. The line through each schedule's median time a message against its walk steps a message has the cost of one walk step for its slope, measured where the engine takes the step; a schedule's walk time is that slope times its steps, and the walk's share is that over its median. The line says nothing about the fixed part of a walk, the side's head and the branch out, which every schedule pays about once a message and which any structure keyed by price would still pay.
