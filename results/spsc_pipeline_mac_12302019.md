# T-SPSC-3: the SPSC ring inside the replay, on macOS (M4 Max), 12302019

Generated by `scripts/spsc_driver.py` from raw records that are not committed. Do not edit by hand.

Host: **macOS (M4 Max)**. Numbers from the two hosts are never mixed.

**macOS (M4 Max) is not a pinned host.** macOS offers no way to pin a thread, so both threads of every row ran wherever the scheduler put them, beside whatever else the machine was doing under `measure.sh`'s lock. Unpinned cross-core latency on the Mac is weak evidence: the two threads may share a cluster or not, and a line moves between P clusters in about three times the time it takes within one (LC-28). The cores each thread last ran on are in the repetitions table. Every figure here is a macOS figure, and every bar below was set on x86, so none is met or missed here.

The whole day three ways on the `paged-flat` book, the shipped arm, each on a fresh book: single, the shipped single-threaded replay, the frame walk and `apply()` on each payload; pipelined, a parser thread that walks the frames, decodes each message the book acts on into a 32-byte op and pushes the ops through the ring, 64 to a publish, to the book thread, which pops them 64 at a time and applies each; and decoded, the pipeline's work on one thread with no handover. The ring holds 65,536 ops. Each pass also hashes the book once, untimed, just after op 131,000,000 of those the book acts on: the day's book is empty again at the close. `bench/pipeline.hpp`.
