- Fees: the base tier of the 2019-12-30 schedule, per fill, in integer picodollars. The fee line is what was paid; a negative fee line is a net rebate.
- S = Σq(m⁻ − P) − Σf, spread capture against the mid just before each fill, its gross and fee lines shown apart. A = Σq(mʰ − m⁻), adverse selection, the mid 15 s after each fill against the mid before it. I = Q₀(m_T − m₀) + Σq(m_T − mʰ), inventory. S + A + I is the total, and it equals the ledger's total, computed apart, with == in every run.
- A tick is $0.01. Per share and ticks a share divide by the run's shares traded, bought plus sold. Each run is a whole day in a fresh process.
- No return and no Sharpe ratio are computed: one day cannot support either.
