# T-BT-3: the backtester with queuetruth's shadow orders, on macOS (M4 Max), 12302019

Generated by `scripts/backtest_driver.py` from raw records that are not committed. Do not edit by hand.

Host: **macOS (M4 Max)**. Numbers from the two hosts are never mixed.

**macOS (M4 Max) is not a pinned host.** macOS offers no way to pin a thread, so each process ran wherever the scheduler put it, beside whatever else the machine was doing under `measure.sh`'s lock. Every figure here is a macOS figure. T-BT-3's bar was set on an Apple M1 Pro under macOS, in Low Power Mode, which its author calls a lower bound for that machine, so a macOS figure is held to it, on a faster Apple part.

queuetruth's workload: 100,000 shadow orders of 100 shares in real FIFO queues on AAPL, MSFT, SPY, TSLA, no strategy, every latency zero. The orders are placed at evenly spaced times over the regular session, cycling through the symbols and the two sides, each at the view book's best price on its side then, and each is cancelled 60 s after it was placed (`ShadowPlacer` in `bench/backtest_workloads.hpp`).

## What an event is

One event here is one ITCH message of the extract through the engine. Before any timing, each process walks the whole day once to count and once to copy the messages of AAPL, MSFT, SPY, TSLA and every System Event into anonymous memory: 5,425,845 of the day's 268,744,780 messages. Only the extract is replayed and timed; the full-day parse and filter is excluded, and its time is in the repetitions table. The denominator is the 5,425,845 extract messages.

queuetruth's 5.19 M records/s is over 6,103,262 records decoded from 5,425,849 ITCH messages of the same four symbols, read from Parquet with a decode thread, so its timed span also excludes the full day. T-BT-3's 4.61 M messages/s is that rate over messages: 5.19 M × 5,425,849 / 6,103,262. The extract here holds 5,425,845 messages against queuetruth's 5,425,849; the rate is compared on messages, and the two counts differ by 4.
