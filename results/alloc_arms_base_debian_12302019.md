# T-ARENA-1: the node book on each allocator arm, on debian, 12302019

Generated by `scripts/alloc_driver.py` from raw records that are not committed. Do not edit by hand.

Rendered again from the run's own raw records, unchanged: on 2026-11-13, by `scripts/alloc_driver.py render` as fixed then, whose header gives as peak_rss the largest timed process over every arm and names its arm; before, it gave the largest repetition of the first arm, malloc-fresh, alone. Only the peak_rss line moved (docs/NOTES.md, "Figures from the raw values, rounded last").

Host: **debian**. Numbers from the two hosts are never mixed.

One book, `include/itch/node_book.hpp`, on each allocator arm of `include/itch/node_arms.hpp`: a linked-levels book whose orders and levels are nodes, each taken from the arm when it opens and given back when it closes, so that the allocator sits on the hot path of every add, delete and replace. Between arms only the allocator changes: the index (16-byte slots, open addressing, sized up front for the capacity), the side table, the walk and the capacity rule are one code. The pool arms hold their nodes by 32-bit index or by pointer, so their node is 24 or 40 bytes; every heap arm holds them by pointer, as the pool by pointer does. Its book matches the fast book's message by message on the day (Book agreement below), and the fast book matches the oracle (`results/oracle_12302019.md`).
