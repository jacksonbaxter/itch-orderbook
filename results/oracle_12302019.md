## T-INV-3: no new cross in normal trading

Verdict: **PASS**, 0 violations. This check is separate from T-INV-2 and does not change it.

The rule, as the user approved it after seeing real data: during normal trading, a book may only be crossed if the cross is left over from a halt and hasn't cleared yet. Any new cross is a failure. The population is market open with the symbol in state `T`, and locked counts apart from crossed. A leftover exists only if that symbol's book is crossed or locked at the moment of a resumption. It ends on the first observation that is neither, or on a cross message. No time limit enters the rule.
