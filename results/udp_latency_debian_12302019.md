## What was run

- ceiling: The sender alone, unpaced, down a pair whose receiving socket nobody reads (`bench_udp --mode sender`): the comparison plan's task 2. No trial runs above 0.9 × its median.
- bisect: Each mode's largest lossless rate by bisection on the message rate, one trial a process, the modes taken in a shuffled order each round. A trial runs min(rate × the trial's seconds, the frame cap) messages; it counts as lossless only when the record is lossless on every counter of IU-43, its verdict is PASS and the sender kept pace, achieving at least 0.99 × the target. From the start rate, halved while lossy; then the cap; then geometric bisection until high over low is at most 1.02 or ten midpoints. Then the best mode at 2,547,190 msg/s, if that is under the cap.
- latency: Fixed rates per mode: a low rate of packets a second, as messages by the bisection's messages a packet, where batching does nothing and wake-up dominates; fractions of the mode's largest lossless rate; and one common rate for every mode, whose processes give the CPU split. Latency runs from each packet's due time on the schedule to the receiver's clock read when the wait that returned it returned, so a sender that falls behind is charged (IU-46); the first second's packets are left out.
