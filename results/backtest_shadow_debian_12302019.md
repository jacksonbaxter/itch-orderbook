## What an event is

One event here is one ITCH message of the extract through the engine. Before any timing, each process walks the whole day once to count and once to copy the messages of AAPL, MSFT, SPY, TSLA and every System Event into anonymous memory: 5,425,845 of the day's 268,744,780 messages. Only the extract is replayed and timed; the full-day parse and filter is excluded, and its time is in the repetitions table. The denominator is the 5,425,845 extract messages.

queuetruth's 5.19 M records/s is over 6,103,262 records decoded from 5,425,849 ITCH messages of the same four symbols, read from Parquet with a decode thread, so its timed span also excludes the full day. T-BT-3's 4.61 M messages/s is that rate over messages: 5.19 M × 5,425,849 / 6,103,262. The extract here holds 5,425,845 messages against queuetruth's 5,425,849; the rate is compared on messages, and the two counts differ by 4.
