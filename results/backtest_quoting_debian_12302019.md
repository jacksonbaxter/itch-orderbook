## What an event is

One event here is one ITCH message of the day through the engine: the frame walk, the exchange-side book, the queue model, and for a traded symbol the delivery to the strategy L_md later, the strategy's decision, and every arrival, fill and report it causes, all inside the one timed span. The denominator is the day's 268,744,780 messages, every symbol's. The strategy sees only its AAPL, MSFT, SPY, TSLA and the System Events, 5,425,845 deliveries; every other message is replayed through the exchange-side book and nothing else. The engine-events rate counts messages, deliveries, arrivals, reports and wakes together over the same span.

LobSim's op, T-BT-1's source, is one event of a synthetic L3 feed through replay plus a market-making strategy, at 507.6 ns an op; its README does not say whether the queue model was on in that row, and its strategy may see every event. So the two rates count a different event on a different feed: ours is a real day on which most messages never reach a strategy.
