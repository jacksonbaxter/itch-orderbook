# T-SPSC-1 and the queue comparison, on debian, 12302019

Generated by `scripts/spsc_driver.py` from raw records that are not committed. Do not edit by hand.

Host: **debian**. Numbers from the two hosts are never mixed.

Every queue of `bench/spsc_queues.hpp` by the four protocols of `bench/spsc_protocols.hpp`, on two threads: rtt, a ping-pong through two queues with one item in flight, the mean over 1,000,000 round trips after a tenth as many untimed; saturated, 100,000,000 sequence numbers pushed as fast as the queue takes them, the first lap of the ring left out of the span; paced, one stamped item every 1 µs and every 10 µs, 200,000 of each after a tenth as many untimed, the one-way latency of each recorded; and burst, the 729,611 messages of the day from 10h after midnight, 34,200,000,000,000 ns for 2 s, each in a 64-byte item with its own length of real bytes, sent at the gaps between their ITCH timestamps, the one-way latency of each recorded. Every queue holds the same element: an 8-byte integer in rtt, saturated and paced, and the 64-byte item in burst. Every queue is asked for the same capacity, 131,072 items. Each third-party queue runs as shipped, through its own non-blocking single-item calls, with its own padding.
