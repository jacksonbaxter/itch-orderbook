# The walks of the backtester's simulated levels, counted, on macOS (M4 Max), 12302019

Generated by `scripts/walk_report.py` from bench_walks records that are not committed. Do not edit by hand.

The engine keeps each side's simulated orders as a list of levels from the best price, and six places walk it from the best: an execution, a cross, a depletion, a late add, a hidden print and a rest (`bench/walk_counts.hpp`). `bench_walks` runs the extract of AAPL, MSFT, SPY, TSLA through a copy of the engine made at build time with a count at each walk (`cmake/ItchWalkEngine.cmake`), beside the engine itself; each schedule's counted engine gave the engine's own stats and event hash. Nothing here is a time. The counts are the same on any host.
