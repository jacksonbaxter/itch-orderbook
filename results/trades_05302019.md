## T-TRADE-3: `E + C_Y + P` against SEC MIDAS

MIDAS gives thousands of shares to three decimals, as text written from doubles, so a value resolves to one share. ITCH gives whole shares. The parser turns the text into whole shares with no floating point, and refuses a value more than a millionth of a share from a whole number. Every comparison is then integer equality, per symbol.

Pinned window: `0935_1600`, half open, 09:35:00 to 16:00:00 in the feed's own timestamps. It is pinned by measurement: the lead was the SEC's method page for its Market Activity Report, which says its sample runs from 9:35 and does not say that of `TradeVol`. Each cell counts symbols of 5,712 that agree exactly.

An odd lot is a print below the round lot of the SEC's rule, from its method page for the Market Activity Report: "Until Nov 3, 2025, a round lot is defined as 100 shares for all but three tickers. BRK.A and SEB have a round lot of one. BH has a round lot of 10."
